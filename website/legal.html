Product boundary

Drip Type composition and typing run on-device. Optional Zap AI sends text and attached screenshots you submit through Zap to NVIDIA for inference. Drip Type drafts remain local unless you choose to submit them to Zap AI. Data from retired local features remains on-device. Account, billing, licensing, support, security, update, AI usage, and ordinary request information is also processed to operate the product. Stripe processes payment information. Vercel hosts the website, first-party Zap accounts, licensing endpoints, support form, and encrypted private account and support-ticket stores; access to stored tickets requires a signed Zap session on the server-side admin allowlist. GitHub and Vercel-hosted storage provide downloadable release files. See the Privacy Policy for the complete data-flow description.
