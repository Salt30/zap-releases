Subscription summary

  • Core and Pro are monthly subscriptions at the price shown before Stripe Checkout confirmation.
  • Drip Type has no free trial or money-back guarantee. Paid access begins only after completed payment.
  • Payments are final and non-refundable except where applicable law requires a remedy.
  • Paid subscriptions renew automatically each month until canceled.
  • Cancel online through the Stripe Customer Portal opened from the app's Billing page.
  • Deleting Drip Type does not cancel a subscription.

Product boundary

Drip Type 1.7 keeps writing workflows on the device and does not use a third-party AI writing API. Stripe processes payment information. Vercel hosts the website, first-party Zap accounts, licensing endpoints, support form, and encrypted private account and support-ticket stores; access to stored tickets requires a signed Zap session on the server-side admin allowlist. GitHub provides downloadable release files. See the Privacy Policy for the complete data-flow description.
