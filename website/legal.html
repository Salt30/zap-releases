Subscription summary

  • Core and Pro are monthly subscriptions at the price shown before Stripe Checkout confirmation.
  • The 14-day local Core trial requires no card and does not automatically convert.
  • Paid subscriptions renew automatically each month until canceled.
  • Cancel online through the Stripe Customer Portal opened from the app's Billing page.
  • Deleting Drip Type does not cancel a subscription.

Product boundary

Drip Type 1.7 keeps writing workflows on the device and does not use a third-party AI writing API. Stripe processes payment information. Vercel hosts the website, licensing endpoints, support form, and private support-ticket store; access to stored tickets requires a verified Zap account on the server-side admin allowlist. GitHub provides downloadable release files. See the Privacy Policy for the complete data-flow description.
