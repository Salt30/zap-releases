Privacy

Drip Type 1.6 keeps composing, templates, fill-in values, settings, and typing on your Mac. It does not use a third-party AI writing API.

Effective August 13, 2026Version 1.2

1. Local writing data

Composer drafts remain in application memory. Templates, typing profiles, explicitly captured clipboard items, appearance, and hotkey preferences are stored locally on the Mac. Filled template values are not saved to templates. Drip Type does not use draft contents to build advertising profiles.

2. macOS permissions

Drip Type requests Accessibility and Automation access so it can enter text into applications you select. macOS controls these permissions, and you can revoke them in System Settings.

3. Updates and network access

The signed desktop application contacts the Drip Type update service to check for new versions. It contacts the subscription service to activate this Mac and periodically verify paid access. Core writing content is not included in update, checkout, or entitlement requests.

4. AI processing

Drip Type 1.6 does not send writing to OpenAI or another third-party AI writing API. Pro Writing Lab transforms are deterministic and on-device. Batch personalization, writing profiles, and the private clipboard workspace do not send writing to a remote service.

5. Billing and licensing

Stripe processes checkout, payment details, invoices, and subscription management under its own privacy terms. Drip Type receives limited subscription identifiers, plan and status information, and device-bound activation hashes needed to provide access. A refresh credential is stored in macOS Keychain. The app does not store card details.

6. Diagnostics

The current application does not operate advertising analytics or writing-content analytics. Operational providers may retain limited request and security logs needed to deliver the website, updates, and entitlement service.

7. Security and retention

We use administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the data processed. No system can promise absolute security. Locally stored templates and preferences remain until you edit, delete, or remove the application data. Stripe retains billing records under its policies and legal obligations.

8. Your choices

You can edit or delete templates, stop a typing session instantly, revoke macOS permissions, manage or cancel a subscription through Stripe’s portal, and stop using the application at any time.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as Drip Type evolves. Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date and, when appropriate, communicated in the product.
