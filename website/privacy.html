Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Drip Type processes on your Mac, what limited information reaches our services, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 16, 2026Version 2.0

1. Who we are

Drip Type and the tryzap.net website are operated by VegaNext LLC under the Zap brand ("Zap," "we," "us," or "our"). For privacy questions or requests, email support@tryzap.net or write to VegaNext LLC, 400 Continental Blvd, 6th Floor, El Segundo, CA 90245, United States.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Drip Type macOS application, tryzap.net pages for Drip Type, subscription activation, software updates, and related support. It does not govern third-party websites, applications into which you choose to type, or Stripe-hosted pages governed by their own notices.

3. The local-first boundary

Drip Type 1.6 does not send your composer drafts, templates, template values, batch rows, writing profiles, explicitly captured clipboard items, or Writing Lab text to Zap, Stripe, Vercel, OpenAI, or another remote writing service. Composer drafts remain in application memory. Templates, settings, profiles, and captured clipboard items are stored locally on your Mac. Clipboard text enters the private workspace only when you select Capture clipboard; Drip Type does not continuously monitor clipboard history.

Writing Lab transforms are deterministic and run on-device. Drip Type 1.6 does not use a third-party AI writing API.

6. Cookies and analytics

The Drip Type pages on tryzap.net do not currently run advertising analytics, cross-site tracking, or writing-content analytics, and we do not set first-party marketing cookies. Stripe-hosted checkout and Customer Portal pages may use cookies or similar technologies under Stripe's Privacy Policy. Hosting and security providers may process ordinary request data needed to deliver and protect the site.

7. When we disclose information

We disclose limited information only as needed to:

  • Stripe, which provides checkout, payment processing, subscriptions, invoices, fraud controls, and the Customer Portal.
  • Vercel, which hosts the website, serverless billing endpoints, operational logs, and signed update files.
  • Apple and your operating system, which provide macOS permissions, Keychain storage, code-signing verification, and notarization checks.
  • Professional advisers, authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud, or handle a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

8. Retention and deletion

Local drafts disappear when their in-memory session ends. Local templates, profiles, preferences, device identifiers, and captured clipboard items remain until you delete them or remove the relevant application data. The subscription refresh credential is stored in macOS Keychain until it is replaced, access is revoked, or you remove it through the app or Keychain.

Billing, subscription, device-hash, support, security, and transaction records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Provider logs and payment records are also subject to the providers' retention practices. When information is no longer required, we delete or de-identify it where reasonably practicable.

9. Security

We use measures designed for the information we process, including HTTPS, Stripe-hosted payment entry, signed and notarized releases, short-lived Ed25519-signed entitlements, hashed device bindings, bounded activation attempts, restricted server credentials, and macOS Keychain storage for the refresh credential. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which law applies, you may have rights to know or access personal information, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a denied request. California residents may also have rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and receive equal service and pricing when exercising their rights. Because we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, no sale/share opt-out is presently required for Drip Type.

For California notice-at-collection purposes, the categories described above are identifiers and customer-record information, commercial information, internet or electronic activity, and support communications. During the preceding 12 months, we collected and disclosed those categories for the business purposes described in Sections 4 through 7, including disclosure to service providers. We did not sell or share them for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Submit a request to support@tryzap.net with the subject "Privacy request." Tell us the right you want to exercise and the email associated with your subscription, if any. 11. International processing

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Drip Type. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
