Refund & Cancellation Policy

Subscriptions should be easy to understand and easy to stop. This policy explains how Drip Type cancellations, renewals, promotions, and refund requests work.

Effective August 22, 2026Version 2.1

1. Cancel online at any time

Sign in to your Zap account and select Manage billing, or open Drip Type, go to Billing, and select the Customer Portal. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and ordinarily takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. You may continue using paid features until then. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

2. Monthly renewals

Core and Pro renew monthly at the price, currency, and taxes disclosed at checkout until you cancel. Cancel before the renewal date shown in the Customer Portal to avoid the next charge. Bank processing and time-zone differences may affect when a pending charge appears.

3. Initial-purchase refund requests

You may request a refund within 14 calendar days after your first paid Drip Type subscription charge if the software does not work as materially described on a supported Mac or Windows device, you were charged more than once for the same subscription, or the purchase was made in error. Use the private support form, choose “Billing or subscription,” and include the subscription email, plan, charge date, and a short explanation. We may ask you to try reasonable troubleshooting steps or provide information needed to locate the charge.

Requests are evaluated under this policy and applicable law. Approval is not guaranteed where substantial use, abuse, fraud, a prior refund, or a non-qualifying reason is present.

4. Renewal charges and partial periods

Renewal charges and unused portions of a billing period are generally non-refundable, and we do not ordinarily prorate a canceled month. We will review duplicate, erroneous, or unauthorized charges and provide any refund required by law. Nothing in this policy limits a non-waivable consumer remedy.

5. Trials and promotions

The 14-day local Core trial requires no card and does not automatically convert to a paid plan. A paid subscription starts only when you explicitly complete Stripe Checkout. Promotion eligibility, duration, redemption limits, and the amount charged after a promotion are displayed at checkout. Unless stated otherwise, promotions have no cash value and cannot be combined.

6. Plan changes

The Customer Portal shows the timing and financial effect of an available plan change before you confirm it. Credits, proration, and the effective date shown there control the change. A plan change does not restart the initial 14-day refund-request window.

7. How approved refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method. We submit approved refunds promptly, but banks and card networks control posting time, which can take several business days. We cannot redirect a refund to a different person or payment method.

8. Charge questions

Use the private support form first so we can identify and address a charge quickly. This request does not waive any right you may have to contact your payment provider or regulator. Knowingly false charge disputes or other payment fraud may result in suspension.

9. Third-party marketplaces

The current Drip Type subscription is sold through Stripe Checkout. If a future purchase is made through Apple or another marketplace, that marketplace's billing, cancellation, and refund rules may control, and the request may need to be submitted directly to it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy prospectively. The version in effect when a charge occurs will ordinarily govern that charge, subject to applicable law.
