Cancellation & No-Refund Policy

Drip Type has no free trial, money-back guarantee, or voluntary refund program. This policy explains paid access, renewals, cancellation, and rights that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.

Effective August 25, 2026Version 3.0

1. Cancel online at any time

Sign in to your Zap account and select Manage billing, or open Drip Type, go to Billing, and select the Customer Portal. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and ordinarily takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. You may continue using paid features until then. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

2. Monthly renewals

Core and Pro renew monthly at the price, currency, and taxes disclosed at checkout until you cancel. Cancel before the renewal date shown in the Customer Portal to avoid the next charge. Bank processing and time-zone differences may affect when a pending charge appears.

3. No trial, money-back guarantee, or voluntary refunds

Drip Type does not provide a free trial. Paid access starts only after you explicitly complete Stripe Checkout and payment succeeds. All payments are final and non-refundable. We do not offer refunds for purchases made in error, dissatisfaction, compatibility issues, failure to cancel before renewal, non-use, or unused time. We do not provide credits or prorated refunds when you cancel or change plans.

4. Mandatory legal rights

Nothing in this policy excludes a refund, repair, replacement, charge correction, withdrawal right, warranty, or other remedy that applicable law makes non-waivable. Where the law requires a remedy, we will provide the remedy the law requires and no broader voluntary guarantee. Use the private support form for an unauthorized or duplicate charge or to assert a mandatory legal right.

5. No promotions that create free access

Drip Type Checkout does not accept promotion codes or provide a zero-cost trial period. Website demonstrations do not activate the application.

6. Plan changes

The Customer Portal shows the timing and financial effect of an available plan change before you confirm it. Any charge, credit, proration, and effective date shown there control the change. A plan change does not create a refund right or money-back guarantee.

7. Legally required payment corrections

If applicable law requires a refund or payment correction, it is returned through Stripe to the original payment method. Banks and card networks control posting time. We cannot redirect it to a different person or payment method.

8. Charge questions

Use the private support form so we can identify and address a charge. Contacting us does not waive a right you cannot legally waive. Knowingly false disputes, account sharing, subscription bypass, or other payment fraud may result in suspension.

9. Third-party marketplaces

The current Drip Type subscription is sold through Stripe Checkout. If a future purchase is made through Apple or another marketplace, that marketplace's billing, cancellation, and refund rules may control, and the request may need to be submitted directly to it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy prospectively. The version in effect when a charge occurs will ordinarily govern that charge, subject to applicable law.
