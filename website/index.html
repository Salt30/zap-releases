Clear answers

Before you start.

What is Zap?+

Zap is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that brings Zap AI and Drip Type together. Zap AI helps you work with text and screenshots; Drip Type enters your finished text into an editable field.

How do I open Drip Type?+

Press Option+5 on Mac or Alt+5 on Windows. Add your text, press Start, then click the destination field during the delay. Option+0 or Alt+0 stops typing. You can change these shortcuts in Zap.

What can Zap AI do?+

Zap AI can answer questions, explain, rewrite, summarize, translate, help with code and form drafts, and create flashcards. Attach a screenshot when you want help with visible content. Review every response before using it.

Do I need my own AI API key?+

No. AI access is provided by Zap through your active subscription. Each subscription has a limit of 100 requests per UTC day and 10 requests per minute. Provider availability and shared service limits also apply.

Does my writing leave my device?+

Drip Type drafts and typing stay on your device. When you choose Ask Zap, your submitted text, custom instructions, and attached screenshots are sent through Zap to NVIDIA to generate a response.

Does Zap AI search the live web?+

No. Research mode creates a brief from material you provide and the model’s existing knowledge. It does not browse the live web or verify current facts.

Which systems and permissions does Zap need?+

Zap supports macOS and Windows 10 or 11. macOS typing requires Accessibility and Automation permissions. Screenshot capture may also require Screen Recording permission. Protected fields and some applications may reject automated input.

How much does Zap cost?+

Zap Core costs $9.99 USD per month. There is no free trial. Existing Pro subscribers can manage their subscription from their Zap account. Cancel through the billing portal to stop future renewals.
